HSV Color Parameters for Post Detection
==================================================

Preto Post:
--------------------
H: 0-180
S: 0-255
V: 0-50

Azul Post:
--------------------
H: 105-125
S: 110-255
V: 85-255

Vermelho Post:
--------------------
Range 1: H=0-10, S=50-255, V=50-255
Range 2: H=170-180, S=50-255, V=50-255

Rosa Post:
--------------------
H: 140-170
S: 50-255
V: 50-255

Notes:
- H (Hue): 0-180 in OpenCV HSV
- S (Saturation): 0-255
- V (Value/Brightness): 0-255
- Red uses two ranges due to hue wrap-around at 0/180
